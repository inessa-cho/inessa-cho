# Inessa Cho

**Prospective graduate student** exploring **robust NLP under distribution shift**, with a focus on how language evolves across time and online communities.

---

## Research Interests

- Robustness in NLP under distribution shift (temporal, domain, community-level)
- Out-of-distribution (OOD) detection in open-world settings
- Language and semantic shift in online environments
- Early signals of emerging patterns through linguistic change

---

## Current Focus

- Studying how language distributions change over time and across communities  

- Exploring embedding-based and LLM-based approaches for:
  - OOD detection  
  - Distribution shift measurement  

- Building small-scale Python tools for:
  - Text similarity and clustering  
  - Exploratory analysis of language variation  

---

## Direction

I’m interested in understanding how NLP systems fail under shifting data distributions, and how to design methods that remain reliable as language and context evolve.
